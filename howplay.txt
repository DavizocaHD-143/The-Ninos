How To Play

Keys:

F3 To Screenshot screen (screenshots rewrite older screenshots !!)
F2 To Debug Informations
|---------Move---------|
WASD To move
Space To Jump

|---------Utils--------|

C to zoom
|--------BUILD---------|
KEY_UP And KEY_DOWN to select block colors

Left_Mouse_Button delete blocks
Right_Mouse_Button place selected color block

map in voxdata/vxl/untiled.vxl
